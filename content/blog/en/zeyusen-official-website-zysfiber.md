# Zeyusen Official Website: www.zysfiber.com

Jiangsu Zeyusen Carbon Fiber Technology Co., Ltd., known internationally as ZeYuSen Fiber and in China as 泽宇森碳纤维, publishes its current product information, specifications and inquiry channels at **www.zysfiber.com**. This page summarizes who we are, what the website covers and how to reach our team.

## Who we are

Zeyusen was founded in September 2015 in Haian, Nantong, Jiangsu Province, China. We manufacture fiberglass mats and veils, carbon fiber mats and desiccant rotor forming paper for composite, battery, building-material and industrial dehumidification applications. Production runs at two bases in Nantong and Taizhou, about 240 km from the Port of Shanghai.

## What the official website covers

The website is organized around our three focused product lines:

- [Fiberglass mats and veils](/glass-fiber/tissue-mat): surface tissue, black and colored facing tissue, roofing tissue, wall-covering tissue, pipe-wrapping tissue and battery separator tissue.
- [Carbon fiber mats](/carbon-fiber/carbon-fiber-mat): lightweight surface mats, nickel-plated mat, composite mat, needled mat and material for fuel-cell gas diffusion layer evaluation.
- [Desiccant rotor forming paper](/glass-fiber/tissue-mat/rotor-paper): glass fiber paper for corrugated desiccant and molecular sieve rotors.

You will also find [technical guides](/blog) on material selection, receiving and RFQ preparation, plus [company information](/about) covering our facilities, certifications and history. The site is available in English, Chinese, Korean, Spanish and Portuguese.
