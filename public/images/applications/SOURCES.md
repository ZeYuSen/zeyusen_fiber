# Application industry imagery — sources & licenses

All images are real industry scene photographs sourced from Wikimedia Commons
under free-for-commercial-use licenses (CC0 / Public Domain / CC BY / CC BY-SA).
Each source was downloaded on 2026-07-11 as a 1920px thumbnail from
`upload.wikimedia.org`, then resized to a 1600px-wide `-hero.webp` (aspect kept)
and an 800×600 4:3 `-card.webp` (attention crop), both re-encoded to WebP to
avoid production hotlinking.

These are third-party industry scenes used as illustrative references
("行业应用示意图"). They do NOT depict ZeYuSen's own factory, equipment, or
project sites. `alt` text is worded accordingly.

## Processing notes

- Source: 1920px `upload.wikimedia.org` thumbnails (per Wikimedia's guidance to
  use listed thumbnail sizes rather than hotlinking originals).
- `-hero.webp`: resized to 1600px width, aspect ratio preserved, WebP q80.
- `-card.webp`: cropped to 800×600 (4:3) with a saliency-based ("attention")
  crop, WebP q78.
- No source is presented as ZeYuSen's own facility.
